Your açaí café in Aberfoyle Park.

Wild and The Sun is a neighbourhood açaí café and coffee shop inside Aberfoyle Hub Shopping Centre, near Woolworths. We serve açaí bowls, smoothies, specialty coffee, croissants, waffles and café food, for locals across Aberfoyle Park, Happy Valley, Flagstaff Hill, Coromandel Valley and Chandlers Hill.

See the full menu

Where is Wild and The Sun?

Wild and The Sun is at Shop 4, Aberfoyle Hub, 130-150 Hub Drive, Aberfoyle Park SA 5159, inside Aberfoyle Hub Shopping Centre near Woolworths. It’s in Aberfoyle Park in Adelaide’s southern suburbs, close to Happy Valley, Flagstaff Hill, Coromandel Valley and Chandlers Hill.

What is an açaí bowl?

Açaí (say ah-sah-EE) is a deep-purple berry from the Amazon with a rich berry and chocolate flavour. We serve it soft and creamy, topped with fresh fruit, granola, nut butters and drizzles. Our Classic Açaí Bowl comes with banana, strawberry, granola and honey, and there’s a Kids Açaí Bowl too.

Is Wild and The Sun only an açaí bar?

No, it’s a full café. As well as açaí bowls and smoothies, there’s espresso coffee and specialty lattes such as pistachio, cookie butter and spiced matcha, Dubai chocolate and pistachio croissants, loaded banana bread, waffles, quesadillas and milkshakes.

What are the opening hours?

We’re open Tuesday to Saturday 9am–5pm and Sunday 9am–2pm, closed Monday. Hours can change on public holidays; check Instagram or message us before making a special trip.

Can I book a table?

Yes. Message us on Instagram or email the café with your preferred day, time and group size. You are also welcome to visit us in person.

Do you host birthdays, events or catering?

Yes. Wild and The Sun welcomes birthday gatherings, special occasions, group tables and catering enquiries. Message us on Instagram or email the café with your date, guest numbers and ideas.

Do you do takeaway or delivery?

Yes. Order ahead for pickup through our online store, or order delivery through Uber Eats.

Are there options for kids and special diets?

There’s a Kids Açaí Bowl and babyccinos for little ones, a veggie quesadilla, and a gluten-free brownie. Please tell the team about any allergies when you order.
