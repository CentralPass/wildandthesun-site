Made with heart. Shared with you.

Wild and The Sun is a neighbourhood café built around the simple pleasure of getting together over something good.

From a quick morning coffee to an afternoon açaí catch-up, we love being part of the everyday moments in Aberfoyle Park. Our little café is a place for familiar faces and new friends alike.

Bring your people together.

Thinking about a birthday catch-up, a group visit or another occasion? Talk with the team about what the café can arrange.

  1. Call us during café hours. We are happy to talk it through.
  2. Share the details. Your preferred date, rough guest numbers and what you are planning.
  3. We confirm with you. Availability, food and drink options, and any pricing or space needs.

Every gathering is different, so no package or capacity is promised until the team has checked the details.

Find us at Aberfoyle Hub.

We’re inside Aberfoyle Hub Shopping Centre, on the corner of Hub Drive and Sandpiper Crescent, near Woolworths. Aberfoyle Park is in Adelaide’s southern suburbs, about 20 km from the city and close to Happy Valley, Flagstaff Hill, Coromandel Valley and Chandlers Hill.
