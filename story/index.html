01 / THE FAMILY

It started with a
little family of three.

Wild & The Sun began with a family who loves wholesome food, generous gatherings and cosy spaces. Their daughter's passion for healthy flavours and vegan living inspired a place where every generation could share natural desserts and colourful drinks.

Friends and relatives gathered at home so often that each visit felt like a small celebration. The café carries that easy welcome forward. Her husband, a skilled handyman and painter, carefully brought the green shop to life by hand.

The family moved to the neighbourhood in 2016 and quickly fell for Aberfoyle Park and its thoughtful community. They wanted the café to feel like an extension of home.
